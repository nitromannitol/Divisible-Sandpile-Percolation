# Scope: Dubins–Dambis–Schwarz (DDS) as a `LatticeProb` project

Status: **scoping only; nothing proved here.** Target: the missing input `(a) DDSRepresentation` of
`ext-bdg` (13 dependents), the lower half of the Burkholder–Davis–Gundy theorem in
`~/lean/Vertex-Reinforced-Walk-Homogenization`. The upper half is unaffected.

## The two targets

**(a) `VRW.BdgLower.DDSRepresentation`** (`wip/BdgLower.lean:48`). For every `Ω`, probability `P`,
filtration `ℱ : Filtration ℝ≥0`, continuous martingale `M : ℝ≥0 → Ω → Fin 1 → ℝ` with `M 0 = 0`,
terminal time `T : ℝ≥0`, and terminal bracket `Q` in the sense `VRW.HasQuadCovar P M T Q`
(`VRW/Model/Martingale.lean:35`: the dyadic realized covariations `Σ_k ΔM ΔMᵀ` at mesh `T/2ⁿ`
converge to `Q` **in probability**), there exist a probability space `(Ω', P')`, a pre-Brownian
motion `B : ℝ≥0 → Ω' → ℝ` and a measurable `τ : Ω' → ℝ` with `τ ≥ 0` a.e. such that

```
P'.map (fun ω => B (Real.toNNReal (τ ω)) ω) = P.map (fun ω => M T ω 0)   -- law of the terminal value
P'.map τ                                    = P.map (fun ω => Q ω 0 0)   -- law of the clock
```

**(b) `VRW.BdgLower.ConditionalGaussianLaw`** (`wip/BdgLower.lean:64`). For every pre-Brownian
motion `B` and measurable `τ ≥ 0`, `B_τ =dist √R · Z` with `Z` standard Gaussian independent of `R`
and `R =dist τ`.

The frozen `VRW.External.DambisDubinsSchwarz` (`VRW/External/DambisDubinsSchwarz.lean`) is strictly
weaker: only the characteristic-function bound `|E e^{iM_T} − e^{−q/2}| ≤ ½ E|Q−q|`. Discharging (a)
is what would let `wip/BdgLower.lean` drop its `DDSRepresentation` hypothesis.

## What is missing (dependency-ordered)

Proposed home: a new `LatticeProb/Prob/` family. Names are proposals to be `grep`-verified against
the pin before use.

### Stage 1 — the bracket process (`LatticeProb/Prob/QuadraticVariation.lean`)

This is the foundational gap; everything else depends on it.

1. `LatticeProb.realizedQVar (M : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ) (t : ℝ≥0) (n : ℕ) : Ω → ℝ`
   — the dyadic sum `Σ_{k<2ⁿ} (M_{t(k+1)/2ⁿ} − M_{tk/2ⁿ})²`, the scalar analogue of
   `VRW.realizedCovar`.
2. `LatticeProb.bracket (M : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ) (ℱ : Filtration ℝ≥0 m₀) (P : Measure Ω) : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ`
   — the quadratic variation process, defined either as the a.e. limit of `realizedQVar` along the
   dyadic mesh or through the Doob–Meyer decomposition of the submartingale `M²`.
3. `bracket_zero`, `bracket_monotone`, `bracket_continuous`, `bracket_adapted`.
4. `Martingale.sq_sub_bracket` — for a continuous square-integrable martingale `M` started at 0,
   `fun t ω => M t ω ^ 2 - bracket M t ω` is a martingale. (For the local-martingale version,
   `IsLocalMartingale` has to be created first; the target only needs the square-integrable case
   at the fixed time `T`.)
5. `tendstoInMeasure_realizedQVar_bracket` — `realizedQVar M t n →ᵢ bracket M t` in measure
   (this is the bridge that turns `VRW.HasQuadCovar` into `Q = bracket M T` a.e.).
6. `bracket_unique` — uniqueness among continuous adapted increasing processes `A` with `A 0 = 0`
   making `M² − A` a martingale.
7. Optional: `bracket_add`, `bracket_const_mul` (bilinearity) for the multivariate statement; the
   1-D target only needs the scalar version.

Cost: the dyadic-convergence construction is the single hardest piece. For a bounded martingale
`M`, `realizedQVar M t n` is a submartingale and the increments `realizedQVar (·) t (n+1) −
realizedQVar (·) t n` are orthogonal in `L²` with summable `L²` norms, giving `L²`-convergence and a
Borel–Cantelli a.e. limit; for a general square-integrable `M` one truncates at level `N` and lets
`N → ∞`. Estimated **1200–2500 lines**.

### Stage 2 — continuous-time optional stopping (`LatticeProb/Prob/OptionalStoppingCont.lean`)

Needed to stop `M` at the bracket inverse.

8. `Martingale.stoppedValue_of_isStoppingTime` — for a continuous martingale `M` and a *bounded*
   stopping time `τ : Ω → ℝ≥0` (i.e. `τ ≤ T`), `∫ M_τ = ∫ M_0` (and the stopped process is a
   martingale). Route: approximate `τ` from above by dyadic stopping times
   (`LatticeProb.dyUp`, `Prob/StoppingDyadic.lean`), apply Mathlib's **discrete** optional stopping
   along the dyadic grid, and pass to the limit using path continuity and uniform integrability /
   dominated convergence. A fully general continuous-time optional stopping theorem is *not* needed;
   bounded stopping times suffice for DDS.
9. `bracket_timeChange` — for a continuous increasing `A` with right-continuous inverse
   `σ_s = inf {t : s < A_t}`, the bracket of `s ↦ M_{σ_s}` is `s`. Specialised to `A = bracket M`.
10. `isStoppingTime_inverseBracket` — `σ_s` is a stopping time. Mathlib has no `isStoppingTime`
    lemma for an infimum of a set; the construction (`{t : s < A_t}` is open in `ℝ≥0` for continuous
    `A`) has to be built.

Estimated **500–1200 lines**.

### Stage 3 — Lévy's characterization (`LatticeProb/Prob/LevyCharacterization.lean`)

11. `isPreBrownianReal_of_bracket_eq` — a continuous martingale `B` started at 0 with bracket
    `bracket B t = t` has `IsPreBrownianReal B P`. Proof: for each `λ`, the process
    `Z_t = exp (i λ B_t + λ² t / 2)` is a martingale (needs a stochastic-exponential / Itô step for
    `f(B_t)`, or a direct polynomial-approximation argument using that `B` and `B² − t` are
    martingales); the martingale property gives
    `E[e^{iλ(B_t − B_s)} | ℱ_s] = e^{−λ²(t−s)/2}`, hence independent Gaussian increments and the
    Brownian fdd.
12. `isBrownianReal_of_bracket_eq` — add the a.e. continuity hypothesis from the time change.

The missing Itô/stochastic-exponential machinery is the second hard piece. Two options, in
increasing cost: (i) a bespoke lemma `martingale_exp_of_bracket_eq` proved by differentiating the
conditional characteristic function of `B_t` using only the martingale property (no general Itô
formula); (ii) a minimal `Itô`-for-`f(B)` lemma for smooth `f`, which is reusable but larger.
Estimated **800–2000 lines**.

### Stage 4 — DDS and the law form (`LatticeProb/Prob/DambisDubinsSchwarz.lean`)

13. `dds_ae_eq` — with `B_s = M_{σ_s}` and `σ` the inverse bracket, `M t = B (bracket M t)` a.e. for
    every `t`. (Uses Stage 2 items 9–10 and Stage 3 item 11.)
14. `dds_law` — the library statement of `VRW.BdgLower.DDSRepresentation`, taking the original
    space as `(Ω', P')` (the time-changed `B` already lives there), which removes the need to build a
    new probability space.
15. `hasQuadCovar_iff_map_bracket` — bridge from `VRW.HasQuadCovar P M T Q` to
    `P.map Q = P.map (bracket M T)`, via Stage 1 item 5. This is the only VRW-specific lemma and can
    live in VRW (`VRW/Model/Martingale.lean`) rather than LatticeProb.

Estimated **400–900 lines**.

### Stage 5 — conditional Gaussian law (`LatticeProb/Prob/ConditionalGaussianLaw.lean`)

16. `conditionalGaussianLaw_of_isPreBrownianReal` — the library form of `(b)`. Specialised to the
    `IsPreBrownianReal` values at a measurable clock; prove via `hasIndepIncrements` plus a
    dyadic/simple-function approximation of `τ` (or, more cleanly, via
    `HasStrongMarkovRestart` for the continuous version and `map_path_eq_of_isPreBrownianReal` to
    transport to the pre-Brownian statement). Estimated **500–1000 lines**.

## Total estimate and risk

| stage | content | estimate (lines) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | bracket process + dyadic convergence | 1200–2500 |
| 2 | continuous-time optional stopping at bounded stopping times | 500–1200 |
| 3 | Lévy's characterization (exp martingale) | 800–2000 |
| 4 | time change + DDS law form + bridge | 400–900 |
| 5 | conditional Gaussian law | 500–1000 |
| | **total** | **3400–7600** |

Risk is concentrated in Stage 1 (a.e. existence of the bracket for square-integrable continuous
martingales, which Mathlib lacks even for the discrete Doob–Meyer decomposition of `M²`) and Stage 3
(the absence of stochastic calculus / Itô). Stages 2 and 5 have templates in tree
(`StoppingDyadic.lean`, `HasStrongMarkovRestart`). This is a **multi-round fleet project**, not a
single worker session.

**Recommended sequencing (independently valuable milestones).**
1. Stage 1 alone: `bracket` + `Martingale.sq_sub_bracket` + `tendstoInMeasure_realizedQVar_bracket`.
   This is the largest single unknown and is useful on its own (the VRW `HasQuadCovar` predicate
   would finally have a process behind it).
2. Stage 2, reusing `StoppingDyadic.lean`.
3. Stage 3, starting with the bespoke `martingale_exp_of_bracket_eq` to avoid full Itô.
4. Stages 4–5.

**Fallback (option (ii) of the status file).** If the author prefers not to spend the rounds,
freeze a new `FROZEN` node for the DDS representation (a citation, exactly like
`VRW.External.DambisDubinsSchwarz`). That is author-gated and out of scope for a worker.
